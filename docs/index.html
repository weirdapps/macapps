Small Mac Apps

Fifty native menu bar apps for macOS. Each does one thing, stays out of the way, and looks like it belongs on a Mac.

Download all Source on GitHub

Free and open source · macOS 14 or later · Apple silicon · First launch help

Native

AppKit and SwiftUI, no web views. Each app is a few hundred kilobytes and idles at zero CPU.

Private

Nothing leaves your Mac. Only Reach and Address touch the network, and only for their one job.

No permissions

No Accessibility, Screen Recording or Automation. Global shortcuts use Carbon hot keys. Only Bin asks for Full Disk Access, to read the Trash.

Out of the way

No Dock icons, no windows, no onboarding. Every app has launch at login in its gear menu.
